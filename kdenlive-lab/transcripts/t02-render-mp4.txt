The lab project (project/garden-tour.kdenlive, written by
scripts/make_lab_project.py) rendered with melt, the same
MLT engine Kdenlive renders with. The command was run from
an unrelated directory on purpose: the project's media
paths are relative, and they resolved.

$ xvfb-run -a melt project/garden-tour.kdenlive \
    -consumer avformat:renders/garden-tour.mp4 \
    vcodec=libx264 pix_fmt=yuv420p acodec=aac ab=128k
(... 150 frames rendered ...)

$ ffprobe renders/garden-tour.mp4
video: codec_name=h264  width=1280  height=720  r_frame_rate=25/1
audio: codec_name=aac   sample_rate=48000  channels=2
format: duration=6.016000  size=180207

150 frames at 25 fps is exactly 6 seconds; the extra
0.016s is the audio stream's padding in the MP4 container.
