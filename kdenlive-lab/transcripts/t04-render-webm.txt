The same project, rendered a second way: WebM.

$ xvfb-run -a melt project/garden-tour.kdenlive \
    -consumer avformat:renders/garden-tour.webm \
    vcodec=libvpx-vp9 pix_fmt=yuv420p acodec=libopus ab=128k

$ ffprobe renders/garden-tour.webm
video: codec_name=vp9
audio: codec_name=opus
format: duration=6.008000  size=170578

Same timeline, same 6 seconds. Only the container and
codecs changed -- the choice chapter 11 walks through.

Sizes side by side:
  garden-tour.mp4    180207 bytes  (H.264 + AAC)
  garden-tour.webm   170578 bytes  (VP9 + Opus)
