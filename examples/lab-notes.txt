The lab -- one real install, one machine, no display
====================================================

The lab is Kdenlive as Ubuntu ships it, installed
with apt on the build machine (an Ubuntu 24.04
sandbox, 2 CPUs, 7.7 GB RAM):

  * kdenlive 23.08.5 and melt 7.22.0 (the MLT
    renderer Kdenlive renders through), plus
    ffmpeg/ffprobe 8.1.2 for making and measuring.
  * The machine has no display. Everything the
    lab verified, it verified through the project
    file and the renderer -- the same machinery
    Kdenlive itself uses -- never through the
    editing window. The window is described from
    the documentation and labelled as such.

The fixture is a six-second film of a fictional
garden tour, invented for the occasion:

  * Two 3-second colour clips with burnt-in labels
    ("Garden gate", "The rose bed"), generated by
    ffmpeg -- they stand in for two camera shots.
  * A 6-second 440 Hz tone standing in for sound.
  * A title clip, "A Short Garden Tour", in
    Kdenlive's own title format (its text layout
    copied from a template Kdenlive ships).
  * One 2-second 3840x2160 test clip, used only
    to measure what proxies save.

What the lab ran, in kdenlive-lab/transcripts/:

  * t00 versions of kdenlive, melt and ffmpeg.
  * t01 the media generated and read back with
    ffprobe (1280x720, 25 fps, 3.0s each; tone 6.0s).
  * t02 a minimal project in Kdenlive's file
    format, written by a script kept in the lab
    (scripts/make_lab_project.py): main bin, three
    timeline tracks, a fade from black and an
    audio fade-in. Rendered with melt to MP4 and
    read back: H.264 1280x720 video, AAC stereo
    audio, 6.016s for a 6-second timeline.
  * t03 the title and both fades measured on the
    render: frame stills show the title over the
    first clip; mean luma climbs 19.7 -> 45.8 ->
    90.7 across the fade; audio RMS climbs from
    -69.4 dB to -24.1 dB across the fade-in.
  * t04 the same project rendered to WebM
    (VP9 + Opus, 6.008s).
  * t05 a project copy with one media path broken:
    the render completes anyway and the missing
    clip's slot is a grey checkerboard.
  * t06 proxy arithmetic on the 4K clip: a
    640x360 proxy is 86x smaller on disk and
    about 17x cheaper to decode.

The sandbox's rules, discovered by running
into them:

  * Titles are drawn by MLT's Qt module, which
    refuses to work without an X11 environment.
    Headless, the title renders as the word
    INVALID in giant letters; under xvfb-run (a
    fake X server) it renders correctly. On a
    desktop this never arises. It is chapter
    16's first entry.
  * The render's duration reads 6.016s, not
    6.000s: the MP4 container pads the audio.
    The video is exactly 150 frames.

Documented only (no display on the build
machine): everything done with the mouse in
the editing window -- placing and trimming
clips, the razor tool, keyframing by hand,
the audio mixer view, title design in the
title window, colour scopes and the
stabilise job. Those chapters describe the
documented behaviour of Kdenlive 23.08 and
say so where it matters. The file-level
facts underneath them -- what a project
contains, what a fade is in the file, what
a render produces -- are the parts the lab
verified.
